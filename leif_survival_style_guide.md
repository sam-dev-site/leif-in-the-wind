# Game Project Master Context & Art Style Guide: Vinland Survival

## 1. High-Level Game Concept

* **Title Concept:** *Leif in the Wind*

* **Core Premise:** Leif Erikson has crash-landed/stranded his expedition on the rugged coast of the New World (Vinland, 11th Century). To survive, he must establish a life, forage, craft, fight, and venture inward toward the mainland of North America.

* **Genre Hybrid:** Top-down open-world survival exploration with procedural generation (*Minecraft* / *No Man's Sky* mechanics) meets cozy life-sim crafting and retro 16-bit RPG styling (*Stardew Valley* / classic *Zelda*).

* **Core Loop:** Free-form exploration, resource gathering, tool crafting, clearing pathways, overcoming environmental/climate obstacles, and light combat.

## 2. World & Environmental Systems

### 2.1 Procedural Generation & Biome Accuracy

* **Non-Linear Navigation:** No forced paths or invisible walls. Players move freely in all 8 directions across open terrain.

* **Realistic North American Biomes:**

  * **Coastal North (Starting Biome):** Dense Boreal forest, spruce, granite outcroppings, cold coastal winds, deep moss, and peat.

  * **Inland River Valleys (Mid-Tier):** Temperate deciduous forests (oak, maple, birch), rich clay beds, arable soil, sluggish riverways.

  * **Interior Mainland (Late-Game):** Mixed grasslands, rolling plains, large game (elk, bison), mineral-rich hills.

  * Interior South (Late-Game): Bayou, swamp, cyprus forest

* **Natural Pathing (Deer Trails):** Organic, winding deer trails cut through dense forests. Walking on these trails reduces stamina consumption; venturing off-path into undergrowth or thick snow slows movement until tools (axes, scythes) are used to clear paths.

### 2.2 Dynamic Progression

* Traversal **Mechanics:** Waterways require crafting rafts or building timber bridges; overgrown brush requires upgrading from stone tools to forged iron axes.

* **Day/Night & Weather Systems:** Dynamic atmosphere shifts affecting visibility, warmth, and lighting (e.g., long pixelated shadows, warm ambient campfire light at night, fog-of-war expansion).

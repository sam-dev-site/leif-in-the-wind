# Skills (Challenge, on this biome)

Status: **woodcutting and fire-making are in.** They show up on `coastal_north` before any new ground. This biome suits them: trees and fallen branches to chop, cold that makes a fire matter.

Do not grow the strip or start `river_valley` instead of this.

## Why this biome

Rocks and logs already block. Night is already cold. Sleep at the fire is already in. The first steps already show every tree, bush, and branch. Getting better at the chop and the light is why this place stays interesting after those first steps.

## Woodcutting (in)

Fir, spruce, and fallen logs share one skill. A branch is still one Space, half a wood, and does not train it. Kindling is the easy gather. Trees are the work.

XP is granted **only when the tree falls**, 25 per fell. One Space and walk away is 0 XP. The remaining chops sit on that standing prop until it falls, **R**, or a load.

Levels 1–30 use Old School RuneScape’s table uncut. After 30 each step costs 3% more than the last. Level 99 is 341,036 XP. Level 92 is about 79% of that, not half.

A workbench mark sits left of the two bars. It opens a short column under those marks: Woodcutting, then Fire-making, each with its level. Hover on a row for a small step chip (`0/83`, then `25/83`) that sits just under the pointer and follows it. Continue still shows name and time. The two bars write `woodcutting_xp` and `firemaking_xp` in that biome’s file. **R** keeps both.

A level-up drops green leaves around Leif and says **Woodcutting is now 2.** (Fire-making uses the same leaves and says **Fire-making is now 2.**)

| | First uses | Comfortable | Maxed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spaces to fell | 3–4 | 2–3 (level 30) | 1 (level 80+) |
| Wood | 0.5 | 3 | 10 |

## Fire-making (in)

**F** spends wood for an attempt on the tile ahead. A miss still spends the wood. The hint is **"The fire does not catch."** XP is granted **only when the fire catches**, **40** per light (more than a fell, because a miss burns a stack).

Catch chance and wood cost share the woodcutting XP table for levels. Cost stays a flat early tax through level 30, then drops.

| Band | Catch | Wood cost |
|---|---|---|
| Levels 1–29 | Climbs from **35%** at 1 toward **95%** at 30 | **7** (flat) |
| Level 30 | **95%** | Still **7** |
| Levels 31–99 | Stays **95%** | Linear from **7 → 1** |

A lit fire still burns one cold night and warms the same pool. Longer burn and a bigger warmth radius stay later ideas, not this Play.

The first fire is fourteen branches, or many clumsy trees. Past 30, cheaper wood is the payoff.

## What we will not do with this

- No XP total on the bottom HUD line
- No skill tree
- Fire-making is signed off in Play. No other skills until asked
- No printing the level on Continue
- No longer burn or bigger warmth radius in this cut

## How this helps the woods

Getting better at the chop and the light is why this biome stays interesting after the first few steps. It does not replace sitting by the fire, and it does not unlock the river valley. A caribou, and then the river, come after this biome is worth staying in.

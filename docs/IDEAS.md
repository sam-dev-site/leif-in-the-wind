# Ideas

Each heading below is its own idea. We talk that one through until its open questions are answered. It stays here until you say that idea is ready to build.

[SLICE_0.md](SLICE_0.md) is what you can play now. [MISSION.md](MISSION.md) still wins if an idea would change the kind of game this is. A new plant or animal needs a research note with sources in `docs/research/biomes/` before it can spawn or get art.

## How a brain dump lands

1. Give it its own heading. If one sentence is two wants, split them.
2. Write what you asked for, what the game does today, the ways it could go, and what we still have to talk about.
3. Leave it. Building is a separate ask, after that conversation.

Notes inside an idea are for the conversation. They are not chosen yet.

## Home screen

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**What you asked for:** A place the game starts, before you are standing in the woods.

**What the game does today:** Play drops Leif on the shore. There is no title, no menu, and no button. **R** makes a new seed in the same session.

**Ways this could go:**

- A title and one button that starts the shore, the way Play works now.
- A front door with more than one choice (continue something, start fresh, hear the woods before you enter).
- A pause menu you can open from the woods, which is a different screen from the one at launch.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- What do you see, and what can you press?
- Is this only at launch, or can you open it from the woods?
- Does “start” always mean a brand-new shore?
- The grove music already plays on the coast. Does the start have sound of its own?

## Profile

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**What you asked for:** Create and save a profile, a user, so a run can exist when the game is closed.

**What the game does today:** Nothing is written to disk. Quit, and the walk is gone. Inside one sitting, **R** keeps the wood and berries you are holding and forgets the map. Fires were on the old ground, so they go too.

**Ways this could go:**

- A name for this run on this PC, and the game remembers that one run.
- Several named runs on this PC (more than one shore you can return to).
- An account that signs in. That is a different product from a file on the computer, and this game is single-player.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- Is a profile a person, or a saved shore?
- One saved run, or several?
- On this computer only, or a sign-in?
- What has to be remembered for it to feel saved: the seed, where he stands, wood, berries, hearts, the map, the time of day, fires still burning, anything else?
- Leif is already Leif. Does creating a profile change how he looks, or only what the save is called?

This idea and the home screen can be talked about apart. A start screen can exist with nothing saved. A save can exist with no title screen. What each one is comes from its own conversation.

## Death and respawn

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**What you asked for:** Leif can die, and something happens after.

**What the game does today:** Night away from a fire spends half a heart every 5 seconds. A full cold night is about six hearts. He has three. The last half-heart stays, so the cold cannot finish him. Day and fire warmth heal him. There is no drowning, no animal attack, and no game-over.

**Ways this could go:**

- The cold can finish him. That is the death already hiding in the night.
- Death is something else (an animal, the water) and the cold keeps stopping at the last half-heart.
- Both.

**After he dies, ways that could go:**

- He wakes on this same shore, somewhere we still have to name.
- He wakes in a new seed.
- The run is over and only a person at the start screen can begin again.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- What is allowed to kill him?
- Where is he when he comes back, and what is still in his hands?
- Does the map he learned stay?
- Three hearts cannot buy a six-heart night. If cold can kill, do we also change how many hearts he has, or how hard the night is? More hearts is its own idea below. It is not decided inside this one.

## Skills and leveling

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**What you asked for:** He gets better at things over time. You called it skills and leveling.

**What the game does today:** One **Space** fells a fir or spruce for 3 wood. **F** always lights if you paid 3 wood. Nothing is gained by doing it again. There is no level anywhere.

**A sketch that already exists:** [SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md) is one picture of woodcutting and fire-making only. In that picture the first chops are clumsy and the fire often fails, and later they feel like the game does today, then a little better. No level number, no skill screen. That file is a sketch for this conversation. It is not the choice.

**Ways this could go:**

- The work itself changes (fewer presses, a surer fire) and you feel it in your hands.
- A number or a bar you can read.
- Many skills, not only the axe and the fire (picking berries, walking, sleeping, something we have not named).
- A level for Leif as a person, separate from any one skill.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- Which activities can improve?
- What does “better” look like while you play?
- Is there a level, and if there is, what is it counting?
- The sketch makes the first hour worse than the game you have signed off. Is that the feeling you want, or should today’s chop and fire stay the beginning?

## Animals

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**What you asked for:** Animals in the woods. This was in the same breath as NPCs. They are split here because a creature and a person are different ideas.

**What the game does today:** No creatures move. The inland path is already a caribou trail. You do not see the caribou.

**What the coast research already allows** (`docs/research/biomes/coastal_north.md`): woodland caribou, arctic hare, beaver, muskrat, red fox, Canada lynx, marten, otter, and on the shore seals and other sea animals. Black bear and the Newfoundland wolf are in that doc as later threats. Moose, coyote, snowshoe hare, and white-tailed deer are the wrong animals for this shore. Anything not in that doc needs research with sources first.

**Ways this could go:**

- You see them. They live in the woods and keep away from you.
- You can hunt them, or they can hurt you. Hurting you meets the death idea only if that idea says an animal is allowed to kill him.
- Tracks, calls, and dung, with no body on screen.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- Which animal, and what is it doing?
- Do you interact, or is it enough that the woods are inhabited?
- A longer coast is a separate idea. An animal can be thought about on the trail that already exists.

## NPCs

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**What you asked for:** NPCs. Read here as people, not the animals above.

**What the game does today:** Leif is alone. No one speaks. The mission says the story is the walk and the land, and that other players are out. A person who lives in the world is still an open idea. A person whose job is to hand you a quest would make the plot the reason to walk, and that fights the mission. If you want that, we talk about the mission on purpose.

**Ways this could go:**

- Someone you see and do not need anything from. A figure on the shore, a camp that is not yours.
- Someone you can talk to, with no task attached.
- Someone who trades, teaches, or travels with you.
- Someone who sends you somewhere. That is the quest case, and it is the one that hits the mission.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- Who are they, in this century, on this coast?
- What do you do when you meet them?
- Are they one person, or people you keep finding?

## Longer coast

**Status:** Ideation. Not in build.

**Where this came from:** Play already runs into a quiet edge at the north of the prebuilt strip. Inland stops. This was the old “next,” and it is an idea of its own, not a gate in front of the others.

**What the game does today:** A seed-built `coastal_north` strip, made once when you press Play. Same forest if you walk north, until it ends. The sea stays south. A new biome is not this idea. `river_valley` is separate, below.

**Ways this could go:**

- More of this same coast, still built before you walk, so the trail continues.
- The edge stays quiet on purpose, farther away.
- The edge becomes a hint of a different land. That hint is how this idea touches the river-valley idea. They are still two conversations.

**Talk through before anyone builds:**

- How far should he be able to walk on this shore?
- When he is far inland, should it still be this forest?

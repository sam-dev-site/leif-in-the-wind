# Coastal sounds

Plan only. Record or find these later. This page does not add audio files.

**The First Grove** already loops as the `coastal_north` bed (`game/art/coastal_north/the_first_grove.mp3`, about three minutes). It starts with the woods, keeps playing through sleep, and does not restart on a new seed. Leave it. These effects sit under that bed. There is no second music track.

Sensation is the job: cold shore, peat, fir. A sound that could be any forest does not belong on this coast.

One file per name when the time comes. Loop files should cycle cleanly. One-shots should end in silence. Jog uses the same step as walk, only closer together.

| Name | When it plays | Kind | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| `step_peat` | Walking or jogging on moss, peat, or the caribou trail. | one-shot | A boot in wet peat and fir needles. |
| `step_shore` | Walking or jogging on the south strand, the landing beach. | one-shot | Fine gravel at the waterline, lighter than the bog. |
| `pick_berry` | Space on bakeapple or blueberry, as the bush comes up. | one-shot | A low bog berry pulled free, wet and close to the ground. |
| `chop_fir` | Space on a standing balsam fir or spruce. One swing fells it. | one-shot | A stone axe in soft coastal conifer: a short resinous crack. |
| `chop_fallen` | Space on a fallen fir or spruce log. | one-shot | The same axe, biting a log that already lies in the peat. |
| `clear_branch` | Space on a fallen fir branch (the small 0.5 wood clutter). | one-shot | A dry twig lifted off needle litter, lighter than the axe. |
| `fire_light` | F, the moment a campfire catches (3 wood spent). | one-shot | Fir and spruce catching in cold air, a small flare on the peat. |
| `fire_burn` | While that fire is lit. One quiet loop per fire. | loop | Low crackle of the same wood, under the grove. |
| `fire_die` | When the fire ends: the night is spent, or sleep burned it to ash. | one-shot | Last coals ticking out on wet peat. |
| `wind_night` | Fades in over the 10 seconds of dusk, holds through the cold night, fades out over the 10 seconds of dawn. | loop | Colder air moving in the firs. The grove keeps playing under it. Day stays as it is. |
| `sleep_blink` | C beside a fire at dusk or night, as the picture fades to black. | one-shot | Eyes closing next to the fire. The grove does not stop. |
| `shore_wind` | On the south landing, while the sea is close. Eases off up the trail. | loop | Cold wind off the strait, with a thin lap on the strand. |

## Stay off this list

Wolves and other animals are not calling. Caribou made the trail; they have no voice in this cut. `river_valley` has no bed and no chops here. Hearts, wood, and forage stay quiet numbers.

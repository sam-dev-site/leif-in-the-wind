# Hunger (later)

Status: **plan only. Not in this slice. Not this Play.**

Bakeapple and blueberry stay in the pack. Forage is carried, not eaten. Wood still buys a fire. This file says what hunger should feel like when we add it. It does not change the coast.

## Why not now

Cold, sleep, and a fire that burns out just signed off. Night away from a fire already spends **half a heart every 5 seconds**. Day, or warmth by the fire, heals **half a heart every 10 seconds**. The last half-heart stays. Death at 0 is still later.

Another drain on those same hearts, in the same cut, would hide which change made the night feel worse. Skills waited for the same reason (`docs/SKILLS.md`). The loop to learn first is: pick the berries, spend wood on a fire, sit the night or walk it.

## What it should feel like (when we do it)

He eats from the pack to stay. A bakeapple or a blueberry he already picked is the meal. One fruit is how a long walk keeps going, and how a long sit stays comfortable. Cooking can come after that bite feels right. The first food is the raw fruit of this coast.

Slice 0 forage here is only bakeapple and blueberry (`docs/research/biomes/coastal_north.md`). Cranberry is in that research file and is not spawned yet, so it is not food until the spawn list changes. Grapes, butternut, and other plants belong to later biomes. They do not grow on this shore to feed him.

The pack stays a count of what he carried. Eating spends one of those. It does not open a menu, a recipe, or a second screen.

## Hearts, cold, and sleep

The night already has one price. Hunger must not double-punish that same night.

- Away from a fire, cold spends half a heart every 5 seconds.
- By the fire, or in the day, he heals half a heart every 10 seconds.
- **C** at the fire sleeps that night. The fire may be ash at dawn.
- The last half-heart stays. Death at 0 waits.

A night he stood in the cold is already paid. A night he slept through is already paid. Sitting by the fire is valid play. Lingering, the blink of sleep, and waking warm are the pastime. Hunger does not tick him down for staying, and it does not turn the camp into a fail state.

When eating exists, it is a reason to use what he foraged so he can stay and so he can go. Cold owns the unsheltered night. Food owns the pack.

## What we will not do

- A hunger bar as a spreadsheet (percents, a meter that is the toy, numbers for their own sake)
- Death this slice, from hunger or from empty hearts
- Eating as the key that unlocks the river valley
- Punishing a long sit, a sleep, or a fire that is doing its job
- Grapes, butternut, or any plant that is not on this coast
- Cranberry as a meal while it is only in the research file
- Cooking in the same cut as the first bite

## How this serves the mission

Survival exists so he can linger after dark and keep going inland (`docs/MISSION.md`). Food in the pack is that: stay, and go. It is not a checklist.

Sensation is the berry he picked on this bog, eaten where he sits. Fantasy is the fruit that grows on this shore. Submission is sitting the night out; hunger has to leave that alone. Discovery is the trail. Food can support the walk. It does not replace the trail, and it does not open the next biome.

The camp stays a rest. Inland stays the spine. Hunger, when it comes, is so both of those still feel possible.

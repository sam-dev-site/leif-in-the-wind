## Where we are

Godot scaffold is in `game/`. Play opens on a title. The coastal still fills the screen. A green box on top of it offers **New Game** and **Continue**. A cream **admin cog** sits in the top right of the title (not the woods HUD). It opens QC sliders for day/night length, hearts, XP curve, foliage %, walk, and run. Save writes `user://admin_settings.json`; session slider values still apply to the next New Game / Continue if he skips Save. Buttons light only while the pointer is on them. Continue is three boxes. An empty one says “No save file” and does nothing. A file shows the name and the Costa Rica time. **New Game** claims the first empty box, fades the menu, turns Leif away from the camera, and he walks inland while that coast is built. Nothing is written until the floppy in the top right. The first click asks for a name. Continue on a real file leaves the same way, and he is where he saved. Chopped trees grow back from the seed. A fire that was still burning comes back with the time it had left. If all three files exist, New Game asks which one to overwrite. The old file stays until that run’s first save. A filled box has a small **x** that asks before it deletes that file. Then you are in the **seed-generated** `coastal_north` woods: shore on the south, caribou trail inland, a prebuilt strip north. Hold WASD/arrows to walk; double-tap the same key and hold to jog. Leif has 8-direction idle **and walk** (jog plays the walk cycle faster). **Space** picks bakeapple/blueberry or chops fir/spruce (stone axe already in hand; a smaller ringed stump stays). The first trees take **3–4** Spaces and pay **0.5 wood**. Woodcutting XP comes only when the tree falls. A skills mark left of the floppy opens Woodcutting and Fire-making. **E** spends 3 forage to heal **half a heart**. A pick still grants 1. Fewer than 3 refuse, and those berries stay. Full hearts refuse, and a bite never goes past max. There is no hunger bar. **F** spends wood (7 at low fire-making) for a campfire attempt. A miss still spends the wood. Catch chance climbs to 95% by level 30; past 30 the wood cost drops toward 1. A catch grants fire-making XP and burns **one night**, then burns out. Night is **60 seconds** of cold (six hearts if you stand it). Away from a fire, hearts drop **half every 5 seconds**; they heal **half every 10 seconds** when he is warm or it is day. The last half-heart stays. **C** by a fire at dusk or night fades to black, then wakes at dawn — the fire may be ash. Fallen logs block their full length. A **tiny bottom-left minimap** opens a small circle around him, and the trail stays clear. Press R for a new seed (wood, forage, woodcutting, and fire-making stay; the walk on the map does not). **Escape** in the woods asks **Quit to the main menu?** Yes / No (and Y / N). Yes returns to the title without closing the game. That is not pause.

How this serves the mission: the trail does not end at a fake wall, and the corner map is the shore he has learned (discovery). The save keeps that shore, the berries and hearts he is carrying, a fire that was still burning, and the skills he has learned (discovery, submission). Three berries buy half a heart back, and a fire is so he can sit the night or keep walking (submission, fantasy). Getting better at the chop and the light is why this biome is worth staying in (challenge). The camp is a rest, not the win. Inland is still the spine; “Leif moved” is not the whole point.

PixelLab trial: **2 / 40** generations left. Then 5/day (stack to 20). Paid floor is Pixel Apprentice **$12/mo for 2,000 gens**. Five gens went to the skills mark (workbench plus a shadow pass), the woodcutting icon, the fir leaf, and the first axe mark. Fire-making uses a placeholder icon until the art lane replaces it.

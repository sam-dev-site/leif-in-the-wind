Play signed foraging off. A skills mark left of the floppy opens Woodcutting, Fire-making, and Foraging. **E** spends forage (10 at low Foraging, down toward 2) to heal **half a heart**. Short hands refuse, and those berries stay. Full hearts refuse, and a bite never goes past max. There is no hunger bar. Quality is the current Foraging level — berries already held use tonight’s cost. **F** spends wood (7 at low fire-making) for a campfire attempt. A miss still spends the wood. Catch chance climbs to 95% by level 30; past 30 the wood cost drops toward 1. A catch grants fire-making XP and burns **one night**, then burns out. Night is **60 seconds** of cold (six hearts if you stand it). Away from a fire, hearts drop **half every 5 seconds**. Day and fire warmth do not heal — berries (**E**) are the only restore. Play signed that off. The cold that would take the last half-heart is **death**: fade to black with a cream skull, wake at dawn at the last slept fire (or the landing), half the wood and berries gone, one heart, skills and map kept. Play signed that off. **C** by a fire at dusk or night fades to black, then wakes at dawn — the fire may be ash. That sleep tile is the death wake place. Fallen logs block their full length. A **tiny bottom-left minimap** opens a small circle around him, and the trail stays clear. Press R for a new seed (wood, forage, woodcutting, fire-making, and foraging stay; the walk on the map and the last-sleep mark do not). **Escape** in the woods asks **Quit to the main menu?** Yes / No (and Y / N). Yes returns to the title without closing the game. That is not pause.
